### AI Innovation Hackathon 2026: From Learning to Impact

**Student Organizer & Problem Setter** · Department of Computer Science & Engineering, Daffodil International University · Daffodil Smart City, Birulia, Savar · 25 July 2026

A Department of CSE initiative led by the CSE AI Centre and seed-funded by the World AI Alliance, convened on the theme *"AI for Cluster Intelligence: Making Compute Faster, Cheaper & More Reliable,"* with support from the Daffodil AI Club, Cyber Security Club, and the AR/VR & Game Development Lab. The competition ran in two phases: 108 teams entered the online qualifying round, of which 65 advanced to the onsite final, each team fielding three members and a dedicated mentor.

- **Problem design:** Authored the problem set around the cluster-intelligence theme, spanning smart scheduling, predictive operations, and cost optimization for GPU clusters. For the final round, designed the Additional Challenge Task methodology used to assess teams live: every team received a bespoke task derived from its own concept note and scoped to five hours, calibrated to extend the system that team had already built rather than to test an unrelated problem. Tasks deliberately permitted AI coding assistants while excluding any dependency on new hardware, large datasets, paid APIs, or extended model training, shifting the assessment away from raw output and toward reasoning, integration, and adaptability under time pressure. Each was issued as a standardised one-page brief setting out the problem statement, background, expected deliverables, and evaluation considerations, written to a plain-language standard so the task read unambiguously for every team. Formally recognised with a Certificate of Appreciation as Problem Setter.
- **Evaluation framework:** Designed the judging rubric and evaluation instrument used in the final, scoring teams on innovation, technical complexity, real-world impact, scalability and sustainability, presentation and demo, team collaboration, and performance on the final-day additional task, together with independent scoring rules and a defined plagiarism-escalation path.
- **Judging panel:** Constituted the judging panel for the final round: 18+ academic judges drawn from Computer Science & Engineering, Software Engineering, and Computing, six industry experts from leading technology companies in Bangladesh, and a Chief Judge from the World AI Alliance. Assigned judges to defined team blocks and evaluation tracks.
- **Competition rulebook:** Authored the official rulebook governing eligibility, team composition, submission requirements, and the two-phase format: concept note, slide deck, and video pitch online, followed by live prototyping and expert pitching onsite.
- **Volunteer operations:** Structured and deployed the volunteer team across registration, team liaison, floor management, and presentation logistics.
